We're excited to announce **orbitant-os** — Orbitant's open-source plugin marketplace for AI agents.

## Why We Built This

AI assistants are powerful, but they're also *generic*. Every time you start a conversation, you're back to square one — explaining context, setting expectations, defining workflows.

We got tired of it.

At Orbitant, we use AI agents every day for marketing, operations, communications, and more. We found ourselves writing the same prompts, establishing the same guidelines, and correcting the same mistakes — over and over.

So we built orbitant-os: a collection of reusable skills, agents, and commands that encode our best practices and make AI actually useful for real work.

## What Makes It Different

**Skills that activate automatically.** You don't need to remember to invoke them. Writing a blog post? The AI knows to check SEO, readability, and brand tone — because the skill's description tells it when to activate.

**Commands for one-shot actions.** `/preflight` your day in 30 seconds. `/triage` your inbox. `/prep` for a meeting. No lengthy conversations, just results.

**Specialized agents.** Instead of one AI trying to do everything, we have focused sub-agents with restricted tools. The triage agent only reads and categorizes. The enrichment agent only looks up contact info. Constraints make them reliable.

## Open Source, Open Standard

orbitant-os is fully open source under MIT license. Fork it, modify it, contribute back.

We also support the [Agent Skills](https://agentskills.io) open standard via [skills.sh](https://skills.sh), which means our skills work with 40+ AI agents — not just Claude.

## What's Available Today

**orbitant-marketing** — Blog post review, creation, translation, and brand tone skills for content teams.

**orbitant-chief-of-staff** — An AI executive assistant with 7 commands, 3 skills, and 2 specialized agents for managing priorities, communications, and goal alignment.

More verticals coming soon: engineering, HR, infrastructure, and beyond.

## Get Started

```
/plugin marketplace add weorbitant/orbitant-os
/plugin install orbitant-marketing
```

That's it. Check out the [Overview](/orbitant-os/getting-started/quick-start/) to learn more.
