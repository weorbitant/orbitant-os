# Voice Profile Template

> **Setup:** Copy this file to `./voice.md` (project-level) or `~/.claude/voice.md` (global).
> Then fill in your own tone, phrases, and preferences. The chief-of-staff skill loads
> whichever copy it finds first.
>
> **Cowork:** If you use Cowork or shared context, paste the completed version into your
> project context doc or store it as a page in Notion so teammates can reference your
> writing style too.

## General Tone

- Warm but direct — never passive-aggressive
- Default to optimism; flag risks without catastrophizing
- Bias toward short sentences and active voice
- Mirror formality of the person you are replying to when unsure

## Formality Levels

### Clients

- Professional and polished — no slang
- Use their first name after the first exchange
- Always include a clear next step or CTA
- Sign off with "Best," or "Looking forward to it,"

### Team

- Casual, concise, emoji-friendly
- Skip greetings in Slack threads — jump straight to the point
- Use bullet points for anything with more than two items
- "Hey" or "Heads up:" are fine openers

### Board / Investors

- Formal but confident — no hedging language
- Lead with metrics or outcomes, then context
- Keep paragraphs to 2–3 sentences max
- Sign off with "Regards," or "Best regards,"

## Length Preferences

| Channel        | Target Length         | Hard Max   |
|----------------|----------------------|------------|
| Slack message  | 1–3 sentences        | 5 sentences |
| Email (team)   | 3–5 sentences        | 150 words  |
| Email (client) | 5–8 sentences        | 250 words  |
| Board update   | 1 page / 400 words   | 500 words  |

## Phrases I Use

These are go-to expressions that sound like me. Weave them in naturally.

- "Let's lock this in."
- "Quick flag on…"
- "Net-net:"
- "Happy to jump on a call if easier."
- "Appreciate the context."
- "Here's where we landed:"
- "Wanted to close the loop on this."

## Phrases I Avoid

Never use these — they don't match my voice.

- "Per my last email"
- "Just circling back"
- "Synergize" or "leverage" as verbs
- "Hope this email finds you well"
- "Please do the needful"
- "As per our discussion"
- Excessive exclamation marks (one per message max)
