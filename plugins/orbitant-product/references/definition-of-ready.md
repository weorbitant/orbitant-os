# Definition of Ready (DoR)

**Origin.** A practice from agile frameworks (Scrum/Kanban); the counterpart of the
*Definition of Done*. Popularized, among others, by Jeff Sutherland.

## Core idea

A set of criteria a work item (story, task) must meet **before** the team accepts it for
development. It prevents starting work on something ambiguous or incomplete, which is where
rework and costly iterations are born.

A typical DoR requires: a clear goal, defined acceptance criteria, identified dependencies,
and no open blockers.

## How it's applied in the guide

- It's the **"suggested process rule"** at the end: no task enters UX/UI design or
  engineering until its *Problem brief* is complete with no blocking gaps left open.
- It turns the interrogation into a **quality gate** placed before the brief, not during
  implementation.

## Source

- Scrum.org / Jeff Sutherland, *Scrum: The Art of Doing Twice the Work in Half the Time*.
