# Problem space vs. solution space

**Origin.** Marty Cagan (*Inspired*, SVPG) and Dan Olsen (*The Lean Product Playbook*)
popularized the distinction, which runs through the whole discipline of product discovery.

## Core idea

- The **problem space** describes the user's need and the outcome they're after,
  **without committing to any implementation**: what hurts, for whom, at what impact.
- The **solution space** is any concrete thing that resolves it: a screen, a flow, a
  feature, a Figma design.

The common mistake is jumping to the solution space before the problem space is well
defined. When that happens, you optimize a solution for a misunderstood problem, and the
gaps surface late (in implementation or in production).

## How it's applied in the guide

- Blocks **1–3** (current situation, problem, impact) force you to inhabit the problem
  space **before** talking about the proposal (block 5).
- The rule "state the problem in one sentence, **without mentioning the solution**" makes
  that separation explicit.
